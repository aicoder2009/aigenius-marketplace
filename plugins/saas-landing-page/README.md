# saas-landing-page

A skill for designing, building, and auditing SaaS landing pages that convert without looking AI-generated.

The core rule: keep UX conventions stable, and make the page different through type, color, voice, and story. Never trade usability for distinctiveness. Claude finds the brand before it writes any code, then works through hero, proof, and CTA structure, plus a checklist of "AI slop" tells to avoid.

It triggers on landing pages, marketing sites, waitlist pages, hero sections, redesign audits, and "make this look less generic".
